About

Brief

I graduated from the Indian Institute of Technology Jodhpur, majoring in CS. My research centers on long-term AI safety and mechanistic interpretability, and understanding how representations form, move, and change inside models.

I lead the technical research wing at Safety and Alignment Research, India, where we're currently field-building India's technical AI safety. Along the way, I've also worked at UofT, Thoughtworks AI Labs, CDA @ UIUC, IAB Lab @ IIT Jodhpur, and CERN engineers.

I am an avid Wordler, (try to) play Chess (@veeraraju_e), watch Cricket, and love Pop and Indie Rock. I'm an ISTJ-A type.
